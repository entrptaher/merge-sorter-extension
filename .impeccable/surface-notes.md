## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact tab utility exposes the four actual operations and their effects immediately. No marketing hero, dashboard, or oversized card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool pale surfaces, a deep forest-green header and action color, compact system typography, consistent line SVG icons, 8px controls, and aligned counts. These serve a browser user under ordinary desktop light. Shared options controls retain the same vocabulary.

STORY: See the current scope and counts, review duplicates when desired, perform a direct operation, and recover the last change. Change defaults only in options.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 400px popup has a small identity/options header, scope control, concise live summary, a duplicate action with expandable review, then group/sort/merge action rows. Feedback and recovery occupy one stable region. No visible control is decorative.

FORM: Grounded candidate 6, folder-label classification, supplies precise text density and site-label grouping only. Seed key `50553dbf`. Native web controls and the user's simplicity constraint govern topology. The signature interaction connects removable duplicate checkboxes directly to the action count.
